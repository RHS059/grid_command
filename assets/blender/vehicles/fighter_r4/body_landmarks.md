The front section has a flat lower face, hard lower chines and a curved roof. The contour includes the local sensor fairing rise.

The generated front guide includes perspective-like overlap. Width and hidden underside shape remain inferred.
The guide notch near the nose gear was removed after comparison with the original photos. The lower body has a continuous flat run and an angled rise.
This file contains only the new body blockout. It is not a final model or export.
